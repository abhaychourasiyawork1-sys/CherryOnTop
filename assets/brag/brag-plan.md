# CherryOnTop — launch film plan

**What it is:** A system for running autonomous AI work as an accountable organization: a goal, a mandate, a budget, and a receipt that proves the result.
**For:** Engineering teams who want AI to do real work without handing it a blank cheque.
**Sets it apart:** Every unit of work has bounded authority and budget, failures are caught by verification, and the outcome is recorded as a Decision Receipt.
**Hook:** One sentence typed into darkness — "Build a customer platform." — then it becomes an organization.
**Visual hook:** The cherry stem's curve is the connector language: the org graph grows out of the same line.
**Real UI reused:** Mission Control's own palette (harbour ground `#131a22`, panel `#1e2a3a`, executing teal `#5fd3c4`, planning lilac `#8c9af0`, at-risk amber `#f2c14e`), node-card anatomy (goal, state, 2px budget meter, `$spent / budget` in mono, breathing ring on executing cards, ⛨ authority mark).
**Tone:** polished / cinematic — long holds, soft fades, one idea per scene.
**Type:** Inter (headlines, labels), JetBrains Mono (figures, logs).
**Caption:** "AI teams you can hold accountable."

## Continuity numbers (fixed across the film)
- Mandate budget $5.00, environment Development.
- Allocation: Frontend $1.40 · Backend $1.40 · Data $1.00 · Verification $0.80 · coordination $0.40
